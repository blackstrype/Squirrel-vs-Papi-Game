HOW TO PLAY - FPS & RAIL PARKOUR

👁️ First-Person Mouse Look

Click anywhere on the screen to lock your mouse pointer. Move the mouse to look around in full 360° FPS view. Press [V] to toggle camera mode.

🌿 Branch Rail Parkour

Climb onto any tree branch, power wire, or fence rail to enter Subway Surfers mode! Press [W/S] to run along the rail, [A/D] to yaw around the branch cylinder, and [Space] to jump off!

👴 Papi FPS Water Hose

Aim with your mouse crosshair and hold [E] or Left Click to spray water hose droplets at raiding squirrels!
